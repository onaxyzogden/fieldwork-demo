# Clarification catalogue

25 common issue types for handyman and specialist intake. Recognition does not establish booking eligibility.

| Issue | Premade questions | Review |
|---|---|---|
| Clogged sink | Which sink is affected?<br>What happens when water drains?<br>Are other sinks, toilets, or drains affected?<br>What has already been tried, including any drain-cleaning product? | Operator review required |
| Clogged bath or shower | Is this a bathtub or shower?<br>Does water drain slowly or remain standing?<br>Are other sinks, toilets, or drains affected?<br>What has already been tried, including any drain-cleaning product? | Operator review required |
| Blocked or overflowing toilet | What is happening?<br>Is water currently spilling onto the floor?<br>Are other sinks, toilets, or drains affected?<br>When did this start, and was anything unusual flushed? | Operator review required |
| Running toilet | Does water run continuously or occasionally?<br>Does the tank refill without flushing?<br>Is there water outside the toilet?<br>How many toilets are affected? | Operator review required |
| Faucet or fixture replacement | What needs replacing, and how many?<br>Are the products and installation hardware already available?<br>What is the model or product link? A photo is also helpful.<br>Are connections or the fixture location changing? | Operator review required |
| Leak under sink | Where is water visible?<br>When does the leak happen?<br>Is the water contained or spreading?<br>Is there visible cabinet or floor damage? | Operator review required |
| Leaking faucet | Which fixture and room?<br>Where does the water appear?<br>Does it happen when the tap is off, on, or both?<br>Would you prefer repair or replacement? | Operator review required |
| Door sticking or not latching | Is it an interior or exterior door?<br>What is the door doing?<br>Is the door or frame visibly damaged?<br>How many doors are affected? | Normal scope checks; reported concerns require review |
| Loose handle, lock, or hinge | Which part is loose or broken?<br>Is it on an interior or exterior door?<br>Can the door currently close and lock?<br>Are the products and installation hardware already available? | Normal scope checks; reported concerns require review |
| Cabinet door or drawer problem | What is wrong?<br>How many doors or drawers are affected?<br>Are hinges, runners, or handles broken or missing?<br>Can you add a photo using the photo button below? | Normal scope checks; reported concerns require review |
| Water stain or damp wall/ceiling | Where is the affected area?<br>What is its current condition?<br>Is the source known or already repaired?<br>Is there visible sagging or bulging? | Operator review required |
| Drywall hole or crack | What kind of damage is it?<br>Approximately how large is it, and how many areas?<br>Is there dampness or staining?<br>What finish would you like? | Normal scope checks; reported concerns require review |
| Painting or touch-up | Which surfaces and how many rooms or areas?<br>What are the approximate dimensions?<br>Are repairs or stain treatment needed first?<br>Is matching paint available? | Normal scope checks; reported concerns require review |
| Shelf installation | How many shelves, and what sizes?<br>What will the shelves hold?<br>What is the wall made of?<br>Are the products and installation hardware already available? | Normal scope checks; reported concerns require review |
| TV mounting | What size and model is the TV?<br>Is a compatible mount available?<br>What is the wall made of?<br>What cable work is requested? | Normal scope checks; reported concerns require review |
| Picture or mirror hanging | How many items need hanging?<br>What are their approximate sizes and weights?<br>What is the wall made of?<br>Are the products and installation hardware already available? | Normal scope checks; reported concerns require review |
| Curtains or blinds | How many windows?<br>What work is needed?<br>Are the products purchased and sized?<br>What is the approximate installation height? | Normal scope checks; reported concerns require review |
| Furniture assembly | Which items need assembling, and how many?<br>Can you provide product links or models?<br>Are all boxes, parts, and instructions available?<br>Is wall anchoring or moving existing furniture needed? | Normal scope checks; reported concerns require review |
| Loose towel bar or bathroom accessory | Which accessory and how many?<br>What is the problem?<br>What is the wall made of?<br>Are the products and installation hardware already available? | Normal scope checks; reported concerns require review |
| Caulk or sealant replacement | Where is the sealant?<br>What is wrong with it?<br>Is there active leakage or soft/damaged material nearby?<br>Approximately how much needs replacing? | Normal scope checks; reported concerns require review |
| Fence or gate repair | Which part needs repair?<br>How many sections are affected?<br>What material is it?<br>Is anything leaning, detached, or unstable? | Normal scope checks; reported concerns require review |
| Deck or step repair | Which parts are affected?<br>How many areas need repair?<br>Is there visible rot, movement, or instability?<br>Can you add an overview photo and a close-up? | Normal scope checks; reported concerns require review |
| Outlet or switch problem | What is wrong?<br>How many are affected?<br>Have you noticed heat, a burning smell, sparks, or discolouration?<br>Is a related breaker repeatedly tripping? | Operator review required |
| Light fixture or ceiling fan | What work is needed?<br>Is there an existing fixture at that location?<br>What is the ceiling height?<br>Is the replacement purchased? Please share its model or link. | Operator review required |
| Breaker, wiring, or circuit request | What change or symptom prompted the request?<br>Which rooms or fixtures are affected?<br>Is the problem ongoing or intermittent?<br>Have you noticed heat, burning smells, sparks, or repeated trips? | Operator review required |

Drain-cleaner answers reveal a product and timing follow-up. Explicit kitchen/bathroom/laundry sink locations and slow drainage are prefilled and editable. Other answers are never guessed. Stable question IDs coexist with legacy saved answers. Unknown issues get item, symptom, and extent questions. No data reset is required.
